HOW TO PLAY

Click a soldier to select. Shift + click to add or remove a soldier.

Click open ground to spend move points. Move next to a blue defender, then click it to spend a fight point and deal melee damage. End the turn to let defenders move and counterattack, restore points, and advance courtyard capture. Health bars show remaining health.

Walls, trees, and rocks beside a ground-level target can block the first ranged hit each turn when they face the shooter. The shot still spends a fight point and reloads. Attack from another direction or use melee to bypass cover.

Select one armed soldier and press Reserve attack to set aside one fight point. The highlighted tiles show where a moving, visible defender can trigger a sword strike or bow shot before it attacks. Press Cancel reaction to reclaim the point before ending the turn. Unused reservations expire when the next turn begins.

Reserve movement to set aside all remaining move points. If a visible defender moves adjacent, the soldier takes one affordable, unoccupied step away before defender attacks. Blue tiles show potential triggers; a blocked or too-costly exit prevents an escape. Cancel before ending the turn to reclaim the points. Unused points expire at turn end. Both reservations can be combined.

Soldiers can start with leather (weight 1, absorbs 1 damage per turn) or mail (weight 3, absorbs 2). Armor protects against melee, ranged hits and catapult stones; cover blocks a shot before armor is spent. Select one armored soldier to remove or re-equip it for one fight point. Protection refreshes on the next turn, but removing and re-equipping does not refresh it. Carried armor slows soldiers on foot.

In the Hamlet, select the first soldier and press Mount to ride the horse on their tile (one fight point). A horse gives 6 base move points without a load penalty, and sword melee deals 2 damage. Mounted soldiers cannot enter houses, use ladders, cross swamps, or crew catapults; mud costs 4 MP. Press Dismount for one fight point to leave the horse on that tile. Hits strike a mounted horse first (2 HP); if it falls the rider survives on foot.

Stand a soldier on a catapult and select them to crew it. Press Aim catapult, then click a target tile (or choose it with arrow keys and press Enter). Aim costs one fight point; Fire costs another, so recruits fire next turn. Each catapult holds three stones and takes two turn transitions to reload. Stones hit anyone on the target tile for two damage, including allies and the king. Two hits on a wall tile breach it, opening a new route.

In the Hamlet, red potions lie on the ground, including one beneath the first soldier and one inside a house. Select a soldier standing on a potion to pick it up (one fight point). Soldiers can carry two potions. Choose a wounded self or adjacent ally in the Heal target selector, then press Heal to restore up to two health (one fight point). Drop a carried potion for one fight point; potions carried by fallen soldiers remain on their tile for recovery.

Fallen soldiers leave their weapon and armor on the ground, even if their armor was removed. Select a soldier standing on gear and choose a piece under Equipment on this tile to recover it (one fight point). Free the matching weapon or armor slot first with Drop weapon or Drop armor (one fight point); recovered armor is worn immediately. All gear counts toward the eight-weight limit. Dropping a weapon leaves a soldier unable to attack until they recover one.

Soldiers can carry up to two tools, each weighing one. Stand on a tool to pick it up for one fight point, or select a carried tool to drop for one fight point. Fallen soldiers leave their tools for recovery. A soldier carrying rope can climb or descend a one-level ledge without a ladder or ramp while on foot; mounted soldiers cannot use it. On night maps, a soldier on or beside a watchfire can extinguish or relight it for one fight point, immediately changing what both sides can see.

Defeating an enemy raises the victor's morale; nearby allies lose morale when a soldier falls. At 0–1 morale, units lose one move point next turn; at 5, they gain one. Select one recruit with 5 morale and press Promote to become a veteran: morale returns to 3, with one extra move and fight point each following turn.

Drag the battlefield to pan. Use the mouse wheel or the zoom buttons to zoom.

Select one soldier and hover over a destination, or focus the battlefield and use the arrow keys, to see the cheapest route. Green steps are available this turn; gold steps need later turns. On foot, roads cost 1 MP, grass 2, mud 3, and swamp 4; unmarked tiles cost 1. Longer-turn estimates assume the path stays clear; group orders spread soldiers to nearby tiles and have no single route preview.

Keyboard: Tab to the battlefield, then use arrow keys to choose a tile. Press Enter or Space to select a soldier, attack a defender, order movement, or confirm an aimed catapult target. Hold Shift while selecting to add or remove a soldier. Tab to the turn controls, including potion actions and their target selector, or the zoom buttons.

Speed is saved on this device. Pause stops animations and prevents new orders, but does not undo an order already sent. Your device’s reduced-motion preference also turns animations off.
